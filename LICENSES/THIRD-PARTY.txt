CASTALIA DOS - THIRD PARTY NOTICES
==================================

Castalia DOS is built on FreeDOS and other free software.  This notice lists
every third-party component that may be present on this medium, its licence,
and where to obtain its source.  It is copied onto release media alongside
the licence texts it refers to.

The original Castalia code (the CASTALIA/BIN tools, the shared modules, the
installer) is MIT-licensed and its source is the project repository.  The
Castalia name, wordmark and fortress artwork are not covered by that grant.

Where the licence text lives on this medium: \CASTALIA\LICENSE\
(GPLv2.txt, and the Artistic Licence as ARTISTIC.TXT).

Where the source lives: the source of the GPL and Artistic components does
not fit on a 1.44 MB disk, so it is published beside the disk image, from the
same place, as castalia-dos-<version>-<codename>-sources.zip.  Its SOURCES\
folder holds one archive per component, named below.  If you were given this
disk without that archive, ask whoever gave it to you for it.


1. FreeDOS kernel  (KERNEL.SYS, and the FAT12 boot sector)
   Licence : GNU General Public License v2 or later  -> GPLv2.txt
   Note    : Castalia ships a MODIFIED kernel.  The changes add a Castalia
             sign-on, the OEM identity 0CAh, the CASTALIA= CONFIG.SYS
             directive, and the INT 2Fh AH=0CAh identity/uptime multiplex.
             Nothing is removed; it remains a superset of FreeDOS.
   Source  : SOURCES\KERNEL-SRC.ZIP (the unmodified upstream source) and
             CASTALIA\patch-kernel-src.py plus CASTALIA\build-kernel.sh (the
