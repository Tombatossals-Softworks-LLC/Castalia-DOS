THE GAME CATALOGUE AND THE NETWORK
==================================

The Game Catalogue (main menu, C) lists games published on the
internet. Pick one, press Enter, and it is downloaded, checked,
unpacked into C:\GAMES and added to the Game Launcher.

WHAT YOU NEED
-------------
A way onto a network that has a "packet driver":
  - an ISA network card (NE2000 or clone, 3Com 3C509) and a
    cable to your router;
  - or a null-modem cable to a modern PC that routes SLIP;
  - or a parallel (LapLink) cable to a PC that routes PLIP.
The drivers for all of these are in C:\CASTALIA\NET.

SETTING IT UP
-------------
Edit C:\CASTALIA\CFG\CASTALIA.INI and fill in [net] driver:

  NE2000 at port 300h, IRQ 10 ..  NE2000.COM 0x60 10 0x300
  3Com 3C509 ...................  3C509.COM 0x60
  SLIP on COM1, 19200 baud .....  ETHERSL.COM 0x60 4 0x3F8 19200
  PLIP on LPT1 .................  PLIP.COM 0x60 7 0x378

Use the IRQ and port your card is set to, and keep them clear of
the sound card (a Sound Blaster usually takes IRQ 5 or 7).

With "dhcp = yes" the router gives the PC an address. Without a
DHCP server, set "dhcp = no" and put IPADDR, NETMASK, GATEWAY and
NAMESERVER lines in C:\CASTALIA\NET\MTCP.CFG.

MEMORY
------
Nothing is loaded at boot. The catalogue loads the driver when it
opens and removes it when you leave, so games keep every byte.

IF IT DOES NOT CONNECT
----------------------
"did not load"     the IRQ or port in driver= is not the card's.
"No address"       check the cable and that the router is on.
"redirected"       the catalogue address must be http://, not
                   https:// - a 386 cannot speak modern TLS.
"damaged"          the download did not arrive whole; try again.

To test the line by hand at the prompt (use your own driver= line
and your router's address):
  SET MTCPCFG=C:\CASTALIA\NET\MTCP.CFG
  C:\CASTALIA\NET\NE2000 0x60 10 0x300
  C:\CASTALIA\NET\DHCP
  C:\CASTALIA\NET\PING 192.168.1.1
  C:\CASTALIA\NET\TERMIN 0x60
